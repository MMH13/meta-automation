# Reel Production Kit — Top Movie Reviews
**3 launch reels · 9:16 · 1080×1920 · no paid video API required**

Every text frame is already rendered in the page brand at `images/reels/`. Filenames carry the assembly order: `r{reel}_{seq}_{type}.png`. You supply only the background stills — prompts below — and assemble in CapCut.

**Total production time: roughly 30–40 minutes per reel the first time, ~15 once you've done one.**

---

## Before you start — the two rules that protect the page

1. **No studio footage, no film clips, no posters inside reels.** Meta's Rights Manager scans video far more aggressively than images. The text frames plus AI stills below are 100% safe.
2. **Audio from Meta Sound Collection only** (inside CapCut: *Audio → Sounds → Facebook*, or download from the Meta Sound Collection site). Meta owns those licences, so Rights Manager cannot claim them. Do **not** use trending TikTok audio — that's the single most common way pages lose monetization eligibility.

## CapCut assembly — do this once, then it's muscle memory

1. **New project → 9:16.** Confirm the canvas is 1080×1920 before anything else.
2. **Drop the background stills** onto the timeline in shot order. Set each to its shot duration from the table.
3. **Add slow motion to every still:** select clip → *Basic → Scale* → keyframe from 100% to 108% across the clip. A static image reads as dead air; a slow push reads as film. **This single step is the difference between amateur and professional.**
4. **Layer the text frames on top** as overlays, matched to the same timings. They're transparent-dark over your stills — set overlay blend to **Normal** and opacity 100%; if a still is too bright behind the text, add a black layer at ~35% opacity between them.
5. **Record the voiceover** (*Audio → Record*). Phone mic is fine in a quiet room with soft furnishings. Speak slower than feels natural.
6. **Add music** from *Audio → Sounds → Facebook*, drag volume to **20–25%**.
7. **Auto-captions:** *Text → Auto captions → English.* Then **check every word** — auto-caption misreads film titles constantly. Position captions in the middle third, never the bottom (Facebook's UI covers it).
8. **Transitions:** none, or a 0.1s dip-to-black between beats. Anything fancier looks dated.
9. **Export:** 1080p, 30fps, high quality.

### Publishing
Post as a **Reel**, not a video. Add the caption above. Keep hashtags in the caption itself, not the first comment — Facebook indexes reel captions.

**Best posting times for US/EU:** 19:00 or 23:00 UTC, matching your existing slots.

## What to expect, honestly

The first few reels will likely get modest views — a new page with 3 followers has no signal history, and Facebook tests content on small audiences before expanding. **Judge them on completion rate, not view count.** If people watch to the end, the algorithm expands the test; that's the mechanism that grows the page.

**Post consistently — one reel per day beats three in one day and none for a week.** Reel distribution rewards regularity more than volume.

If a reel does break out, the static posts already in your queue are what convert those visitors into followers. That's why both halves matter — but the reels have to come first.
